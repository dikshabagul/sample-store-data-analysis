# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis (Excel Project)

This project analyzes the sales data of **Vrinda Store** using **Microsoft Excel** to generate insights about customer behavior, sales trends, and channel performance. The main objective is to build an **interactive dashboard** and derive insights that can help improve business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

Vrinda Store wants to create an **annual sales report for 2022** to understand its customers and identify strategies to increase sales in the coming year.

The analysis focuses on:
- Sales trends across months
- Customer demographics
- Top-performing sales channels
- Regional sales performance
- Product category performance

---

# 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains order and sales information for Vrinda Store including:

- Order ID  
- Order Date  
- Gender  
- Age Group  
- State  
- Sales Channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.)  
- Product Category  
- Order Status  
- Sales Amount

# ❓ Business Questions (KPIs)

The analysis answers several key business questions:

1. Compare **Sales vs Orders** using a single chart  
2. Identify the **highest sales month**  
3. Determine **who purchases more: Men or Women**  
4. Analyze **different order statuses**  
5. Find **Top 10 states contributing to sales**  
6. Understand the **relationship between age and gender** based on orders  
7. Identify **sales channels generating the highest revenue**  
8. Determine the **highest selling product category**

# 📊 Key Insights

- Women customers contribute around **~65% of total purchases**  
- The **30–49 age group** contributes the highest number of orders  
- **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh** are the top states in terms of sales  
- **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** are the top-performing sales channels  
- Sales peak during certain months, indicating seasonal trends

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Target **women customers aged 30–49 years**
- Focus marketing efforts in **top performing states**
- Promote products through **high-performing channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra)**
- Launch seasonal campaigns during high-demand months
